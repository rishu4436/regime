# Binance Web3 API and Agentic Wallet

*Part of the Developer Experience Report for **Regime**, our autonomous tokenized-stock agent (BNB Hack: Tokenized Stocks). Written 11 Oct 2026 by the
exchange-layer owner (`backend/exchange`). We only list what we hit ourselves. For each item we say whether the evidence is
a **live call**, the **`baw` 1.10.0 CLI source/help**, or the **docs**. Most of this was written before the first real-funds swap. Regime's first
autonomous mainnet trade (10 USDT into NVDAB on 11 Oct 2026, 09:25 IST) is [0x228c…9f22 on BscScan](https://bscscan.com/tx/0x228c02688cd2a8170b38dba6abfdb4f95fca9e9e22ae6bed6a191d0b88a19f22).*

## What we built on

- **Web3 API** (`web3_api.py`, `signing.py`): HMAC-SHA256, Base64 over `timestamp + METHOD + /build/path?query + body`.
  We use candles, `rwa/search`, `rwa/price`, `/aggregator/quote` and the swap/RFQ paths. SIM, PAPER and LIVE clients sit behind one
  `ExchangeClient` interface (`factory.py`).
- **Keyless RWA status** (`www.binance.com/bapi/.../rwa/asset/market/status/ai`, documented in the Skills Hub
  `binance-tokenized-securities-info` skill): per-token `openState / marketStatus / reasonCode` (`status.py`).
- **Agentic Wallet** through the `baw` 1.10.0 CLI as the LIVE executor (`baw.py`, `baw_executor.py`), plus a read-only
  smoke check (`baw_smoke.py`). The client is built `read_only=True` and refuses `market-order swap` and every other write command.

## Pain points: what happened, impact, our workaround, suggested fix

| # | What happened | Impact | What we built | Suggested fix for Binance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Region block.** From our US-hosted build box, every Web3 API call returned **40304** "Service not available due to compliance restriction" with **HTTP 200** (live). The `baw` backend (`www.binance.com/bapi`) is behind the same kind of gate. | We couldn't verify anything with real data from CI or the shared box. All real-data checks ran from the team lead's PC in India, through a stdlib-only copy of our signer and a GET-only allowlist. | Region-independent unit tests on recorded responses. Live checks are scripted for the India PC. | A sandbox/testnet host without the region gate, and a real HTTP 451/403 instead of 200 with an error body. |
| 2 | **Onboarding: there is no testnet**, and only the auth errors told us whether our signing was right: a wrong secret gives **40102 Invalid signature**, a bogus key **40101** (live). | The first real trade is the first end-to-end test of the money path. | Transaction-API-style dry runs, quote-only checks, SIM mode with the API's real rules (30 s quote TTL, routing, minimums). | A testnet or a "validate only" flag on `/swap` and `/order/submit`, plus a signature-check echo endpoint. |
| 3 | **xStock quotes suddenly required `userWalletAddress`.** A TSLAx quote without it returned **40001** "userWalletAddress is required for RFQ (xStock) quote" (live). The route is RFQ even though we only wanted a price. | PAPER mode (real data, simulated fills) broke for xStock. Price discovery now needs a wallet address. | A throwaway **quote-only wallet** (unfunded, never signs) via `BINANCE_WALLET_ADDRESS`. The client fails fast with a typed `QuoteUnavailable(code=40001)` (`live.py`). | Allow anonymous indicative RFQ quotes, or say clearly in the docs which families need a wallet address. |
| 4 | **"TRADING" is not the same as liquid.** The status endpoint said TSLAx `openState=true, reasonCode=TRADING`, but a 25 USDT quote returned **40374 RWA_INSUFFICIENT_LIQUIDITY** on Sunday (live). Its newest 1 h candle was about a week old. | Status alone would have made the agent try to trade a token nobody quotes. | `TradingStatus` combines status, candle staleness and zero-volume tails (`status.py`). **xStock is off by default in LIVE.** The quote is the final check. | A liquidity/quotable flag (or a last-quote timestamp) in the market-status response. |
| 5 | **Minimum order size is only discoverable through an error.** Ondo returns **40375 ONDO_FROM_USD_AMOUNT_TOO_SMALL** ("Minimum order amount is 20 USD"). No response field gives a minimum for any family (docs + samples). | Risk can't size an order without first failing a quote. | `min_order_usdt` per token: 20 USD for Ondo from 40375, otherwise our 10 USDT team floor (marked as an assumption in `status.py`). | `minOrderUsd` / `maxOrderUsd` on the token list, status and quote responses. |
| 6 | **Off-hours is ambiguous.** On the weekend NVDAon reports `marketStatus="offhours"` and is still quotable via a DEX SWAP route, but its 1 h candles have volume `"0"` and sparse bars (live). bStock reports `marketStatus=null` 24/7. | Indicators built on zero-volume bars mislead the strategy. Weekend prices track a frozen reference. | Status `OFFHOURS` ("reference-price-only") when the market status says so, or when the last 6 bars have zero volume. The engine stays out of volume-based signals. | Document what `offhours` means per family, and mark synthetic/zero-volume bars in the candles. |
| 7 | **Error codes instead of reasons.** Market closed (40367 Ondo / 40369 bStock), no liquidity (40374), below the minimum (40375) and above the maximum (40366) all arrive as quote errors. | Normal situations look like failures, and the loop treated them as exceptions. | `quote_or_reason()` returns a quote **or** a structured reason (`MARKET_CLOSED`, `INSUFFICIENT_LIQUIDITY`, `BELOW_MINIMUM`, …) with no exception, shared with risk and the explainer. | A non-error "not tradable now" quote result with a reason and `nextOpenTime`. |
| 8 | **Price impact exists only on the Web3 quote.** `/aggregator/quote` has `priceImpactPercent`, but `baw market-order quote --json` returns only `fromCoinAmount / toCoinAmount / slippage`. The CLI strips `quoteId`, fee and gas from its JSON output (CLI source). | The route that actually executes (`baw`) carries no impact figure, so risk needs a second API and a second key. | `price_impact_pct` from the Web3 route when present, otherwise quote price vs the current token price. We always pass an **explicit `--slippage <risk cap %>`**, never `auto`. | Return `priceImpact`, `referencePrice` and `quoteId` in `baw` quote JSON, and let `swap` take a `--quoteId`. |
| 9 | **QR-only sign-in and silent session expiry** (up to `maxSigninDuration`, e.g. 48 h, plus inactivity sign-out; docs + `wallet settings`). | A "24/7 autonomous" agent still needs a human to re-scan the QR. | Preflight reads `sessionExpireTime`. `SESSION_EXPIRED`/`NOT_LOGGED_IN` → auto-pause plus a re-sign-in alert. | A long-lived, revocable agent credential scoped by the App limits, and an expiry warning event. |
| 10 | **Wallet limits are only partly readable.** There's no per-transaction cap at all. The allowed-token list can't be read; only the `tradeAllTokens` boolean is exposed. Docs don't say whether `dailyLimit` counts **sells** (CLI source + docs). | We can't check a token before quoting it, and a stop-loss sell might be refused once the quota is used up. | Our own per-order cap (`BINANCE_LIVE_MAX_ORDER_USDT`, default 25). Risk trades under the lower of the wallet's `quotaLeft` and its own caps and keeps quota in reserve for exits. `allowed_tokens: "LIMITED (list not exposed)"` in `/api/wallet`. | Expose a per-tx cap and the allowlist in `wallet settings`, and document (or exempt) sells in the quota. |
| 11 | **`abnormalTxnHandling` must be `AutoReject` for autonomy.** With `NeedConfirmation`, a flagged trade waits for an App tap and the wallet reports `tx-lock: LOCKED` (docs). | A pending review would block every later trade, including stop-loss sells. | `/api/wallet` warns when it's `NeedConfirmation`, and the go-live checklist requires AutoReject. `APP_CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED` → `PENDING_CONFIRMATION`, never retried. | Don't let a pending review lock exits, or offer a per-direction setting. |
| 12 | **Generic error text for quota and policy rejections.** These come back as `ORDER_API_ERROR` (30003001) with the server's message passed through, so there's no dedicated quota code (CLI source). | Robust handling depends on matching message text. | `classify()` in `baw.py` maps codes first, then keywords (`quota_exceeded`, `policy_rejected`, `slippage_exceeded`, …). | Stable machine-readable codes for daily-limit, token-scope and risk-control rejections. |
| 13 | **Fills: `market-order list` has no received amount, and `wallet tx-history --tx` returns status only** (no amounts or gas, per the CLI source). The full `tx-history` list items carry `instructions.send/receive` and `networkFee`. | Getting the real fill price and gas takes an extra paged query. | `_real_fill()` reads amounts and gas from the matching `tx-history` list item and falls back to the quote, labelled by source. | Put `toTokenActualQty`, gas and fee on the `market-order list` row and on `tx-history --tx`. |
| 14 | **The CLI is awkward to drive from code.** It has no HTTP timeout (plain `fetch`), errors are printed as JSON to **stdout** with exit code 1, and there's no idempotency key on `swap` (CLI source). On Windows, npm's `baw.cmd` can't be launched by `subprocess` without a shell. | A hung call or a timeout after submission could mean a double order. | Our own timeouts (reads 20 s, swap 45 s, polling 120 s). A **process-wide single-flight lock**. An **intent journal** written before every swap. Unknown outcomes are **never retried**: we reconcile via `market-order list --fromToken --toToken --startTime` and return **`PENDING_CONFIRMATION`** until then. `BAW_CMD` / `node <npm prefix>\…\dist\index.js` resolution on Windows. | A `--timeout` flag, a client idempotency key (`--clientOrderId`), and errors on stderr. |

## What worked well

- The Skills Hub `binance-agentic-wallet` skill is a clear execution contract (always `--json`, an orderId is not a fill,
  poll until finished, never downgrade a limit order to a market order). We built the executor straight from it.
- The keyless RWA market-status endpoints made "can this token trade right now?" cheap and region-tolerant.
- `baw` routes Ondo, bStock and xStock internally (a dedicated Ondo path, display-unit quantities with the shares multiplier), so we
  never had to handle EIP-712 RFQ signing on the wallet side.
- The Web3 auth errors were precise (40101 vs 40102), so we confirmed our signer on the first live call.
